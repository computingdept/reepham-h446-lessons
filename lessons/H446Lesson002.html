Question: Subtract the normalised values 011010 0011 − 010010 0010. Both use a 6-bit mantissa and 4-bit exponent. Show the answer in the same format. [6 marks]

Adapted from OCR H446/01 · June 2025 · Q3(c)

Compare exponents

The exponents are 0011 (+3) and 0010 (+2), so the second number must be aligned.

Align the second mantissa

Arithmetic right shift once and raise its exponent: 010010 0010 → 001001 0011.

Subtract and check

011010 − 001001 = 010001. The result begins 01, so it is normalised.

Mantissa
The signed significant binary digits.
Exponent
The signed power of two applied to the mantissa.
Two’s complement
A representation for positive and negative binary values.
Align
Make both exponents equal before arithmetic.
Arithmetic right shift
Shift right while copying the sign bit.
Sign bit
The leftmost bit that indicates the sign.
Normalised
Stored without avoidable leading sign bits.
Positive pattern
A normalised positive mantissa starts 01.
Negative pattern
A normalised negative mantissa starts 10.
Precision
The number of significant bits stored.
Overflow
A result outside the representable range.
Rounding error
Lost accuracy when shifted bits cannot be stored.
